# DEVOID

## Overview

**DEVOID** is an arcade-style third-person shooter set in space. The game combines a retro neon wireframe aesthetic with smooth modern animations and visual effects.

The player pilots a spaceship tasked with cleaning up the remains of a rogue military AI. Gameplay focuses on fast-paced space combat, responsive movement, aiming, and high-score chasing.

## Gameplay

The two core mechanics are **flying and shooting**. Movement is designed to be fast and responsive, while combat rewards players who can effectively aim, dodge, position themselves, and learn enemy movement patterns.

Players can choose between four distinct spaceship classes, each providing a different combination of statistics, weapons, and playstyles:

* **Assault** — A balanced class suitable for close- to medium-range combat. It has average health, speed, and maneuverability, making it the recommended class for learning the game.
* **Heavy** — A durable class equipped with a powerful splash-damage cannon. Its lower speed and maneuverability, combined with a slow-firing weapon, encourage careful positioning and accurate aiming.
* **Interceptor** — A highly maneuverable close-range class equipped with high-damage shotguns. Its low health and poor damage at long range encourage aggressive, fast-paced combat.
* **Sniper** — A long-range class equipped with a high-velocity railgun. Its high speed and long effective range allow players to engage enemies from a distance and use shoot-and-run tactics.

The primary interaction with enemies is straightforward: approach them to trigger their aggression, then destroy them using the player's weapons.

## Objective and Scoring

DEVOID does not have a traditional victory condition. Instead, it is structured as an arcade game centered around achieving the **highest score possible**.

The run ends when the player's health reaches zero. The player must then restart with a score of zero, while their previous high score remains saved between sessions.

## Player Feedback

Combat provides the player with information about enemy positions, enemy health, attack effectiveness, and incoming danger. This information is communicated through:

* Enemy markers
* Dynamic health bars
* Predictive target indicators
* Responsive sound effects

These systems are intended to provide the information necessary for making fast decisions during combat.

Movement and combat are designed to create a tense, reactive experience where players must constantly evade attacks while attempting to outmaneuver enemies. Responsive controls and learnable enemy movement patterns allow skilled players to improve through practice.

## Visual Style

The game uses a **retro neon wireframe aesthetic** combined with modern animation and visual effects. The visual direction is intended to appeal to players interested in science-fiction and retro-inspired games.
